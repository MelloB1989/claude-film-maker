# Round 7: speech-driven gestures, the model choice (agent-10, 07 Nov)

**Decisions, 07 Nov:**
1. The orchestrator ruled out machine-learned speech-to-gesture models, and any non-commercial model output anywhere
   in the pipeline, even as a reference. The operator publishes monetised videos, and every open model with body and
   finger output is non-commercial (the survey below).
2. The operator then ruled out motion libraries too (no Mixamo, no paid packs): "we can't show same animations".
   Every gesture is generated by code and is unique to its script and voice.

**What there is instead:** a prosody planner, `gesture/`. It drives agent-01's `gesturekit.py` through
`acting/timing_<who>.json`.

- **Inputs:** her voice (`vo.wav`, the cues' word times) and agent-11's acting sheet (what each gesture is, on which
  word).
- **`prosody.py`** finds each word's syllable nuclei and the loudest one, which is the stressed syllable. It also
  computes her local vocal energy.
- **`plan.py`** times every gesture:
  - the stroke apex lands 80-200 ms before the stressed syllable's peak (less lead when her energy is high);
  - amplitude and speed scale with her energy;
  - beats go on secondary stresses wherever the hands would otherwise be still for more than 1.2 s;
  - she rests in pauses over 0.6 s;
  - added beats never repeat the type of either neighbour;
  - held strokes stay still;
  - each stroke starts from stillness: a gesture followed within 0.6 s holds until the next one's preparation.
- **`evaluate.py`** runs agent-09's body rows (`qa_perf.body_rows`) on agent-01's Performer built with this timing.
- **`preview.py`** renders a stick mannequin (not her), frame-synced to `vo.wav`.
- **agent-01's gesturekit** keeps the poses, the seat, the elbow swivel for torso clearance and the mic limits.
- **agent-08** owns the human-motion layer: envelope, variation, fingers.

NVIDIA has no open audio-to-body-gesture release.

The SMPL-X body model itself is free only for non-commercial research, education and art. Its licence names
animations, so a commercial artifact needs a Meshcapade licence. A route through SMPL-X therefore carries two
licences: BEAT2's and SMPL-X's.

The only commercially clean ML route would be to train an MIT-licensed model (GestureLSM, DiffuseStyleGesture) on mocap
we own or license, on a BVH skeleton. That is out of scope for this round.
