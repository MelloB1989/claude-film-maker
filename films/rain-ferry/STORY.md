# 雨渡し — The Rain Ferry

Episode 1, 「一閃」 *One Flash*. An original ink-wash anime short of about two minutes, made as a trial of the film
engine's anime pipeline. Everything in it, story, characters and designs, is original. It is meant to share a mood
with the wandering-swordsman manga it was inspired by, not to adapt or borrow from any of them.

## Logline

A hungry young ronin who wants to be the strongest under heaven demands that an old ferrywoman take him across the
river, to the legendary swordsman who is said to live there. She will not cross while it rains. He draws his sword on
her, and in one flash he learns who the legend was and what strength is.

## Characters

**Shinza (新左), 19**: a ronin from nowhere. Tall, lean, sun-dark; sharp hungry eyes; black hair cut short and ragged
(cut with a blade), held off his face by a faded **vermilion headband**; a thin pale scar through his left eyebrow.
Patched charcoal-grey kimono with the sleeves tied back by a cord, tattered dark hakama, straw sandals, a wide straw
kasa hat. A long plain-black-sheathed nodachi slung across his back, the hilt over his right shoulder. He is all
appetite and pride: he talks in short, rough lines and grins when he is threatened.

**Tsuru (鶴), about 70**: the ferrywoman. Small and wiry, perfectly upright. White hair in a low bun held with a plain
wooden pin, a deeply lined face, calm half-lidded eyes that are sharper than they look, a faint smile. A straw rain
cape (mino) over a faded indigo kimono, straw sandals. A long thin kiseru pipe. Her **bamboo ferry pole** is
taller than she is and wrapped near the top with worn cord; it hides a straight, thin blade (a shikomizue). Forty
years ago she was the swordsman the river valley called **the River Demon (川鬼)**.

**The crow**: a big rain-soaked crow that watches the crossing. It opens and closes the film.

## Look

- **Ink-wash anime**: brush outlines of varying weight, sumi ink washes, dry-brush texture; flat cel shading on the
  characters, painted backgrounds with depth, soft mist. Never photographic.
- **Colour arc**: the film starts almost monochrome (cold slate blue, grey, paper white, deep ink black) with one
  accent, the vermilion of Shinza's headband and the hut's lantern. When the rain stops the light turns warm gold,
  and colour comes back into the world.
- **Rain is a character**: it never stops until the moment it stops. It freezes in the air at the duel.
- 16:9, 24 fps. 2.39 letterbox bars slide in for the duel and go back out for the ending.

## Sound

Rain carries the film: its density follows the drama and it cuts to silence at the freeze. The score is sparse and
synthesized in code: a breathy end-blown flute, plucked koto, taiko and low drones; a heartbeat in the standoff; a
warm koto and string swell when the light comes. All of the sound effects are synthesized too.
