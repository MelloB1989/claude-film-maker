# Your Life As a Lighthouse Keeper: credits

- **Story, drawings, edit:** made with this repo: the drawings by `blender/draw.py` and `blender/lib/toon.py` from the
  shapes in `blender/draw/*.py`, composited and cut by the engine.
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs, "George - Warm, Captivating Storyteller" (`JBFqnCBsd6RMkjVDRZzb`, premade),
  `eleven_multilingual_v2`; credits spent are in `audio/credits.log`.
- **Music:** a procedural temp score, `film-temptrack --style gentle` (numpy, no samples).
- **Sound effects:** procedural, `film-ambience` (numpy, no samples).
- **Font:** Patrick Hand by Patrick Wagesreiter, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/PatrickHand-Regular.ttf`, from
  google/fonts `ofl/patrickhand`).
- **Style reference:** the flat-cartoon look of the "Your Life As..." explainers (e.g. Lost Legacy's "Your Life As
  North Korean"); nothing of theirs is used.
